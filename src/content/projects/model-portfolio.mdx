<Section id="problem" label="The problem" title="Standard portfolio templates flatten every model into the same grid">

A model is judged on the same thing the portfolio is — how it looks. Standard portfolio templates flatten everyone into one identical, sterile square grid.

</Section>

<Section id="solution" label="Solution" title="Organise the site around style sets, not one gallery">

The showcase is structured around her distinct style sets and editorial moods rather than a single flat gallery, so her aesthetic range becomes the focal point.

<Insights>
<Insight label="01" title="Style sets, not one gallery">
Organised around distinct editorial style sets instead of one uniform gallery.
</Insight>
<Insight label="02" title="Range at first glance">
A dynamic layout built so range and versatility read immediately in the first viewport.
</Insight>
<Insight label="03" title="Motion with weight">
Silky image transitions and spring-physics media viewing.
</Insight>
