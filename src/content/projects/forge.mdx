<Section id="problem" label="The problem" title="Faith apps felt like homework, and churches couldn't reach anyone in the moment">

Content arrived as long pages of text. When a church asked for prayer, there was no way for young people to see it, let alone answer while it still mattered.

<Insights>
<Insight title="Static text walls">
Long pages of scripture and notes, with nothing built for how young people actually read on a phone.
</Insight>
<Insight title="No live connection">
A prayer request reached nobody until the next service.
</Insight>
<Insight title="Drifting handoffs">
The designs and the shipped app slowly stopped matching each other.
</Insight>
<Insight title="Scattered admin">
Churches juggled separate tools to post devotionals and moderate their community.
</Insight>
</Insights>

</Section>

<Section id="solution" label="Solution" title="One ecosystem instead of scattered tools">

A Flutter app for members, a React portal for churches, and one API behind both: live prayer over Socket.IO, sermons that keep playing in the background, and design tokens that come straight from Figma.

<Section id="architecture" label="How it's built" title="Three tiers, each with one clear job">

<Diagram
  caption="How Forge fits together: app, admin portal, API, and the live pieces between them."
  placeholder="System diagram"
/>

The app and the portal both talk to one Express API. It decides who may do what, keeps everything in MongoDB, pushes new prayers over Socket.IO, and serves sermon media through a Multer pipeline on Render. The app itself is Flutter with Riverpod, Material 3 and a background audio player; the portal is React 19 with Vite and Tailwind.

</Section>

<Section id="decisions" label="Design decisions" title="Four choices that shaped how Forge feels">

<Choice title="Design tokens flow straight from Figma into Flutter">

A Python script (`extract_tokens.py`) parses raw Figma JSON into Flutter `AppSpacing`, `AppColors` and typography constants, so spacing, colour and type come from the same place as the designs.

<Takeaway title="Why it matters">
Design changes reach the app through the pipeline instead of being re-typed by hand.
</Takeaway>

</Choice>

<Choice title="Prayer requests arrive the moment they're shared">

Socket.IO channels carry live prayer counters and group request chats, so young people can respond while it still matters, not at the next service.

</Choice>

<Choice title="Sermons keep playing when the app is in the background">

Background audio (`just_audio` and `audio_service`) and video playback (`chewie`) make a sermon something you can take with you on the walk home.

</Choice>

<Choice title="What you can do follows your role">

Refresh token rotation and role-based permissions for members, leaders and admins, so a church can hand out the portal without handing over everything.

</Choice>

</Section>
