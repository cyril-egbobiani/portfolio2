<Section id="problem" label="The problem" title="Joining an agency means guessing how it really works">

When you join a new agency you're mostly guessing — what they really do, how they work, and how organised they actually are.

<Insights>
<Insight label="Insight #1" title="You're guessing from the outside">
What an agency does day to day, and how well it runs, is hard to see before you're inside.
</Insight>
<Insight label="Insight #2" title="General answers don't help">
Ask a general chatbot and you get a confident summary of nothing.
</Insight>
</Insights>

</Section>

<Section id="solution" label="Solution" title="Go narrow instead of general">

Structured breakdowns of specific agencies — workflow, structure, and how they actually operate. Being genuinely useful about a small thing beats being vague about everything.

<Takeaway title="The principle">
A small, accountable scope over a generic answer engine.
</Takeaway>

</Section>

<Section id="what-it-covers" label="What it covers" title="Each breakdown answers what you'd ask on day one">

<Insights>
<Insight label="01" title="Workflow, hierarchy, and health">
Structured agency breakdowns covering workflow, hierarchy, and operational health.
</Insight>
<Insight label="02" title="Narrow and accountable">
Deliberately narrow and accountable rather than a generic answer engine.
</Insight>
<Insight label="03" title="Made for people moving roles">
Built for designers and engineers entering the field or moving between agencies.
</Insight>
</Insights>
