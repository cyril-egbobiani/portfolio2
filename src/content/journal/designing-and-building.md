I personally believe that some of us need to wake up and face the fact that we are our own greatest enemy. It's a hard pill to swallow, but it's the truth.

I'm currently 21 and I can say that I've made a lot of mistakes not because I needed to learn, but because I was just genuinely afraid.

## The answer has always been within you
What is that fear? can't you see that this "emotion" is a trap?[^1]



- Pay the price!
- Read that book, study that course!
- Express your feelings to that opposite gender! 
- Actually accept your destiny and do what is necessary to get there!!

## Will you be broken in the process? Possibly! Will you cry? Possibly.

But  keep` on pushing, there where so many times I thought I was wasting my strength learning design even tho I had a B.Sc in Software engineering. But I knew where I was going, I knew where I wanted to see myself. I told myself that I will build and design, I will be the one to build and design. Even tho you don't see the results yet, doesn't mean you have to stop.

## What nobody tells you

The race is not to the swift! No matter how fast you start, it doesn't determine the outcome, No matter how slow you start, it doesn't determine the outcome. It's about consistency! It's about never giving up! It's about finishing the race! [^3]

## Why I keep doing it

When I'm working on Forge, I can have an idea over breakfast and hold it in my hand, running on my phone, by the evening. I don't think I'll ever get tired of that.

Design taught me to care about the person on the other side of the screen. Engineering taught me to respect what it takes to put something there. I'd like to keep getting better at both, and I'll keep writing about it here as I go.
